# MediConnect delivery checklist

- Sticky navigation includes the MediConnect logo, Features, How it works, Doctors, and a primary Get started action; it gains a subtle blurred background on scroll.
- Hero includes an eyebrow, a headline of no more than 10 words, supporting copy, primary and secondary CTAs, and a visual showing continuity of patient history over time.
- Features section contains Smart Booking, Digital Prescriptions, Unified Health Timeline, and Automated Reminders, each with an icon and one-line description.
- How it works presents Book, Get Prescribed, and Track & Follow Up as three numbered steps laid out horizontally on desktop.
- Doctors section contains four cards with names, specializations, years of experience, clinic names, and ratings.
- Dark trust/stats band contains three counted-up statistics.
- Final CTA precedes a footer with copyright, About, Contact, and Privacy links.
- Interactions are restrained: once-only scroll reveals, subtle button lift/shadow hover, and doctor-card lift hover; no flashy particle, 3D, or cursor-following effects.
- Responsive layout stacks into a single column on mobile with generous section padding.
- Exact palette and typography roles are used; no red, purple, bright blue, stock-photo doctor imagery, testimonials, or fabricated review quotes are included.
